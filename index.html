01 The condition

Most houses fail in the gap between the render and the pour.

Conventional delivery

  1. iAn architect draws what a builder must reinterpret.
  2. iiThe bid is a guess dressed as a number.
  3. iiiEvery unknown becomes a change order, priced after you are committed.
  4. ivThe finish date drifts by a season, then by a year.

The Massif method

We removed the handoff. The engineer who sizes the beam sits eleven metres from the shop that welds it and the superintendent who sets it. Nothing is translated, so nothing is lost — and the price we hand you in month two is the price you pay in month twenty.

  • 1 contract, drawings through keys
  • 0 change orders billed since 2016
  • 18 weeks of forensics before a shovel

02 Capabilities

Four trades, one roof.

Most firms subcontract the parts that decide whether a house is beautiful in year forty. We kept those in the building.

  1. 01 Site forensics Geology, drainage, sun paths and eighteen weeks of listening to the land before a dollar moves.
  2. 02 Structure In-house engineers. Every beam drawn by the people who will stand under it while it is set.
  3. 03 Fabrication Our Oakland shop — blackened steel, rift walnut, board-formed concrete, glass set to a millimetre.
  4. 04 Delivery One superintendent from excavation to keys. One number. One date, written into the contract.
