# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Young people and families in Saudi Arabia planning a weekend kashta: an evening or overnight trip to the desert. They browse on their phones, compare price, neighborhood and what each place includes, then want to book fast and be sure the spot is theirs.

Secondary audiences already served by the product: place owners (camp and session operators) who list places, block dates and track bookings and payouts, and platform admins who approve places, manage roles and moderate reviews.

## Product Purpose

Kashtat is a marketplace for booking kashta spots, camps, chalets and hourly sessions. A good outcome is a user who goes from "we want to go out this weekend" to a confirmed booking in one sitting, with no double booking and no back-and-forth with the owner.

## Positioning

Kashtat has no separate positioning claim yet beyond the marketplace itself. These are the mechanisms it has today:
- Overlapping bookings are blocked in the database itself (`bookings_no_overlap`, setup time included), so a booking the user sees confirmed is really theirs.
- Prices are calculated server-side from the place's price table, never in the browser.
- It covers several booking types in one place: desert kashtas, equipped camps for one night or more, and hourly sessions.

## Operating Context

- Launch and marketing focus on Riyadh first. The platform already supports other cities (city pages under `/cities/[slug]`, demo places in other regions) and will expand.
- Users search by city, type and dates, open a place page, and book. They manage their bookings at `/bookings` and can cancel before the start time.
- Owners work from `/owner`: add a place, block periods, see bookings and payouts. Admins work from `/admin`.
- Search traffic targets queries like «كشتات الرياض» via city pages and guides.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack in place: Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind 4, Supabase (Postgres + Auth + RLS), deployed on Vercel at https://kashtat.vercel.app.
- Amounts are stored in halalas and displayed in SAR via `formatSar`.
- Booking statuses: pending, confirmed, checked_in, checked_out, completed, cancelled. Each one has its own word in the UI; color is never the only signal.
- **Target booking model (decided): instant booking + full online payment.** The current build is a training version that confirms instantly with no payment (`book_now`). Payment is not built yet. The payment provider is not chosen yet.
- Terminology: كشتة (desert outing), جلسة (bookable session/unit), مخيم (camp). «عربون» (deposit) is not part of the target model; see Brand Commitments.
- Arabic-first, RTL. Dark theme is the default; light theme exists. The theme choice is saved in `localStorage` and applied before first paint.
- Undecided: the payment provider, the refund and cancellation policy once payment exists, and the order in which cities after Riyadh roll out.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: «كشتات» / «الكشتات». There is no logo yet: write the name as text and do not draw a stand-in logo.
- Voice: simple, direct Gulf Arabic, for example «احجز كشتتك». No emoji. Line icons.
- Trust before beauty: the neighborhood is always shown, but the exact location and owner contact appear only after a booking is confirmed.
- A design system for Kashtat was supplied by the owner (Nov 2026: tokens, components, README; style references Solid State, Creative, Now UI Landing, Corona for layout only). It is not committed to this repo yet. Its copy assumes a request/deposit flow («أرسل طلب حجز»، «بانتظار العربون»), which conflicts with the decided instant + full-payment model; product truth here wins over that copy. Visual decisions belong to new-work/DESIGN.md, not here.

## Evidence on Hand

- Demo place photos in `public/places/`, generated by `scripts/place-images/`. They are placeholders, not real camps.
- City SEO copy in `src/lib/city-content.ts`; guides in `src/lib/guides.ts`; keyword notes in `docs/seo-keywords.md`.
- Missing, do not fabricate: real customer testimonials, booking counts, partner camps, press, ratings beyond what the `reviews` table holds, and real camp photography.

## Product Principles

1. Book with certainty: what the user sees as available can be booked, and once confirmed, the spot is theirs.
2. Speed on the phone: the path from search to a confirmed booking stays short and works one-handed.
3. Riyadh first, without cutting off growth: lead with Riyadh, but keep cities a data concept, not a hard-coded assumption.
4. Privacy until commitment: exact location and owner contact stay hidden until the booking is confirmed.
5. Status in words: every state reads as a word, with color as support only.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Arabic RTL is the primary experience. Status must never rely on color alone. A visible focus ring is required on every surface, in both themes.
