# Design System: آفاق الفكر

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Monochrome Stage"**

آفاق الفكر is a bookshop that gets out of the way of its books. The interface is a quiet stage in black, white and one studio grey; every book cover sits on that grey like a product shot in a photographer's sweep, and the covers carry all the colour on the page. Navigation, filters, buttons, cards and footer stay monochrome. The result reads like a well-kept shelf card in a good bookshop, not a retail ad: dense but orderly, confident without hype.

The system is Arabic first and right-to-left. Layout is written in logical properties so it mirrors cleanly, and Arabic text is given room to breathe (display leading opens to 1.25 so letters never clip). Containers are square and flat; controls are round. Depth comes from tone and hairlines, never from shadows under cards. Type is a billboard-and-catalogue pairing: a condensed display face appears only for campaign lines set inside imagery, and a neutral sans carries everything else at a deliberate jump from 32px headers down to 16px catalogue text.

Motion is restrained and functional: state changes are fast and in place, entrances land softly, and the only showpiece is a book cover that tilts toward the reader on hover. All motion collapses to zero under reduced-motion preferences.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Canvas and Soft Cloud cover about 95% of every screen; colour lives in book covers and editorial imagery.
- Every book image sits on Soft Cloud at 4:5, edge to edge in its card.
- Black pill buttons, one primary per screen.
- Square, shadowless containers; fully round controls.
- An 8px spacing grid, with section rhythm stepping 48 / 32 / 24px across desktop, tablet and mobile.
- RTL and Arabic first; logical properties only.

## Colors

A monochrome retail chrome (Ink, Canvas, one studio grey) with a disciplined set of semantic and editorial accents that never touch the chrome.

### Primary
- **Ink** (#111111): The only brand colour. Primary CTA fill, active filter chip, headings, body text, focus ring, and the hairline a shelf of covers stands on. Reads 18.9:1 on Canvas and 17.3:1 on Soft Cloud.
- **On Primary** (#ffffff): Text and icons on Ink fills: the primary pill, the selected chip, the member-benefit card.

### Secondary
- **Soft Cloud** (#f5f5f5): The studio grey. Backdrop behind every book image, the search pill, the secondary pill, the utility bar, and full-width tonal bands that separate a section without a rule.
- **Bronze** (#897a62): Sampled from the gold title on the house book cover. An editorial accent for book-cover moments only: a cover title, a small release-date mark beside a forthcoming title. 4.52:1 on Ink; prefer it at 24px and up when used as text.

### Tertiary
Collection accents. Small fills, swatch dots, soft tiles and editorial washes only; never text, never a primary button, never chrome.
- **Accent Teal** (#0a7281): Editorial accent in collection lockups.
- **Accent Pink** (#ed1aa0): Small chip fills and swatch dots only.
- **Accent Pink Soft** (#ffb0dd): Soft tile tint in member tiles.
- **Accent Purple Soft** (#beaffd): Editorial swatch dot, soft category chip.
- **Accent Purple Pale** (#d6d1ff): The lightest soft-tile fill.
- **Accent Pink Deep** (#4c012d): Deep editorial wash over heritage tiles.

### Neutral
- **Canvas** (#ffffff): Page background, product-card container, on-image pill fill.
- **Charcoal** (#39393b): Softer body text where Ink is too heavy (11.5:1 on Canvas): ledes, FAQ answers, supporting copy. Never a button fill.
- **Ash** (#4b4b4d): Low-emphasis utility text on Canvas (8.7:1), and the outline of unselected filter chips, where a control needs a boundary that Hairline cannot give.
- **Mute** (#707072): Author and category lines, metadata, footer links, column headers in comparison tables, struck-through prices, and "not included" marks. On Canvas (4.94:1) or Soft Cloud (4.53:1) only.
- **Stone** (#9e9ea0): Secondary text on Ink only (7.1:1). It fails on light grounds (2.67:1).
- **Hairline** (#cacacb): 1px decorative dividers: filter rows, footer columns, disclosure rows, table rows, the frame around a tier grid. Only 1.64:1 on Canvas.
- **Hairline Soft** (#e5e5e5): The inset line under sticky bars and tab strips; the hover fill of the secondary pill.

### Semantic
- **Sale** (#d30005): The discounted price and its % off text, the only red in retail chrome. 5.57:1 on Canvas. **Sale Deep** (#780700) is its pressed state and its form on dark grounds.
- **Success** (#007d48): Confirmations, in stock, eligibility ticks; always paired with a word or icon. 5.21:1 on Canvas. **Success Bright** (#1eaa52) is success on Ink.
- **Info** (#1151ff): An informational link or badge in member callouts. 5.76:1 on Canvas. **Info Deep** (#0034e3) is its pressed state.

### Named Rules
**The Monochrome Stage Rule.** Chrome is Ink, Canvas and Soft Cloud. If a nav, filter, button, card or footer carries a hue, it is wrong; the colour belongs to the covers.

**The Price-Line Red Rule.** Sale red appears on the price line and nowhere else: never a background, never a badge fill, never a heading.

**The Bronze Belongs to Books Rule.** Bronze marks book-cover moments only. It is never chrome and never a CTA.

**The Stone-on-Ink Rule.** Stone is legible only on Ink. On a light ground, use Mute.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bebas Neue, with Noto Kufi Arabic for Arabic (then Inter, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Inter, with IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic (then Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif)

**Character:** A condensed poster face for the one campaign line, against a calm, even-weighted sans that does all the reading work. The sans sits almost entirely at weight 500, so hierarchy comes from size and colour rather than boldness.

### Hierarchy
- **Display Campaign** (500, 96px, 0.9): The Latin campaign headline set inside hero imagery, uppercase. Steps to 64px on tablet and 48px on mobile.
- **Display Campaign Arabic** (800, 96px, 1.25): The Arabic campaign headline at the same size and steps; leading opened so Arabic letters are not clipped. Also used for the title on a typographic stand-in cover.
- **Heading XL** (500, 32px, 1.2): Section headers and the book title block on the product page.
- **Heading LG** (500, 24px, 1.2): Subsections, member-benefit titles, the product-page price, and a section lede in Charcoal.
- **Heading MD** (500, 16px, 1.75): Card titles, FAQ row labels, filter group headers, tier names.
- **Body MD** (400, 16px, 1.5): Default body, search placeholder, book description. Running answers cap at about 68ch.
- **Body Strong** (500, 16px, 1.5): Book name on cards, filter labels, primary nav links.
- **Link MD** (500, 16px, 1.75): Underlined inline link (1px underline, 4px offset; 2px on hover).
- **Button LG / MD / SM** (500; 24px / 16px / 14px; 1.2 / 1.5 / 1.5): Hero CTA, standard pills and chips, compact pill and geo selector.
- **Caption MD** (500, 14px, 1.5): Author, category, release labels, filter counts, footer links, usually in Mute.
- **Caption SM** (500, 12px, 1.5): Badge text, utility bar, chip counts, estimate footnotes.
- **Utility XS** (500, 9px, 1.75): The legal fine-print row only.

Numerals are lining; prices and any figure that changes in place use tabular lining numerals so columns do not jitter. Prices are written `39.99 ر.س`.

### Named Rules
**The Zero-Tracking Rule.** Letter spacing is always 0.

**The Display-In-Image Rule.** Display faces are for campaign headlines inside imagery (or a cover stand-in), never a section title or a book name. Uppercase applies only to Latin display lines.

**The Billboard-and-Catalogue Rule.** The jump from 32px headers to 16px catalogue text is deliberate. Don't fill it with intermediate sizes.

## Layout

Content sits in a centred column with a 1440px maximum width and fluid side gutters (clamp(16px, 4vw, 48px) on the membership surface). Spacing follows the 8px grid tokens; the vertical gap between major sections is 48px on desktop, 32px at 1023px and below, and 24px at 599px and below, and generous closing sections may take one and a half times that rhythm.

Breakpoints are ultrawide 1920+, desktop 1440, tablet 1023, tablet-narrow 960 and mobile 599. The book grid runs 3 columns on desktop, 2 below 1023px and 1 below 599px, with 8px gutters (4px on mobile). Listing pages carry a 220px filter sidebar. The page rhythm is one campaign tile per row, followed by a 2- or 4-book grid.

A horizontal shelf is the alternative to a grid: covers in a single row that scrolls inline with mandatory snap, aligned to the content gutter at its start and running off the inline-end edge on desktop, with the next cover peeking on mobile. Two-column editorial splits (a heading and controls against a large result, or a heading against an FAQ list) collapse to one column at 1023px.

Everything is written in logical properties (inline-start / inline-end, block-start / block-end); nothing uses left or right.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Cards, tiles, images, nav and footer sit on the page with no shadow and no lift. Depth is made by tone (an image on Soft Cloud inside a Canvas card; a full-width Soft Cloud band between Canvas sections) and by 1px hairlines. There are exactly two shadow tokens, and neither is a drop shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Inset Hairline** (`box-shadow: inset 0 -1px 0 #e5e5e5`): The bottom edge of sticky utility, primary-nav and sub-nav bars. The only shadow on chrome.
- **Focus Halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 12px #f5f5f5`): A soft 12px halo around the focused search pill, paired with a 2px Ink border.

### Named Rules
**The Two-Shadow Rule.** If a shadow is not the inset hairline or the search focus halo, it does not belong. Emphasis comes from an Ink outline or a tonal band, not from elevation.

## Shapes

The form language is square containers and round controls. Every container (card, tile, book image, tier panel, nav, footer, tonal band) has square corners (0px). Every control and token-sized mark (pill buttons, chips, badges, swatch dots, the 40px circular icon button) is fully round (9999px). Two intermediate radii exist for exactly two jobs: the search pill and input (24px) and the avatar or icon container in member-benefit lockups (18px). A 30px radius is recorded for the CTA pill corner as the source describes it, but pills are built fully round.

Borders are 1px. Hairline is decorative and never the only boundary of a control; an interactive outline uses Ash or Ink. A featured or selected container is marked by a 2px Ink outline, not by a fill or shadow. Icons are outlined 24×24 line icons at a 1.5px stroke in currentColor, with rounded caps and joins.

### Named Rules
**The Square Container, Round Control Rule.** If it holds content, its corners are square. If you press it, it is a pill or a circle.

## Components

### Buttons
Quiet, confident pills. One clear next step per screen.
- **Shape:** Fully round (9999px), 48px minimum height in the membership build.
- **Primary:** Ink fill with On Primary text, Button MD type, 12px block and 28px inline padding. One per screen.
- **Secondary:** Soft Cloud fill with Ink text; hover deepens to Hairline Soft. At most one alongside the primary.
- **On Image:** Canvas fill with Ink text, placed at the bottom inline-start corner of a campaign tile.
- **Icon:** A 40px circle with an Ink glyph.
- **Hover / Focus / Press:** Colour changes run at 200ms on the standard ease; press scales to 0.98 at 100ms on the press ease. Focus is a 2px Ink outline at a 2px offset (Canvas on Ink).
- **Text links** are the only other action style: underlined Body-weight 500 text.

### Chips
- **Style:** Canvas fill, 1px Ash outline, Ink text in Button MD, fully round, 44px minimum height with 8px block and 24px inline padding.
- **State:** Hover fills Soft Cloud; selected flips to an Ink fill with On Primary text and an Ink outline (100ms). Chips built as radio groups keep the native input for keyboard and screen readers, and show the focus ring on the visible pill.

### Badges
- **Style:** A small round outline pill: 1px border, Caption SM type, 4px block and 12px inline padding, no fill. Used for a promo or plan label next to a name.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** Square (0px).
- **Background:** Canvas card; the book image sits on Soft Cloud at 4:5, filling the card edge to edge.
- **Shadow Strategy:** None (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** None on product cards. A group of tier panels is framed in Hairline with Hairline dividers between panels; the featured panel takes a 2px Ink outline.
- **Internal Padding:** 0 on product cards; name, subtitle and price stack with 8px gaps beneath the image. Tier panels use 32px padding (24px on mobile).

### Inputs / Fields
- **Search Pill:** Soft Cloud fill, 24px radius, Body MD text and placeholder.
- **Focus:** A 2px Ink border with the 12px Soft Cloud focus halo, at 200ms.

### Navigation
- **Primary Nav:** The wordmark set as text in the sans at weight 700 at the inline-start, then Body Strong links. The active item is the one underlined nav element; there are no fills or pills in the nav.
- **Utility Bar:** Soft Cloud, Caption SM text.
- **Sticky bars** close with the inset hairline. On small screens, secondary links drop away and the active item remains.

### Disclosure Rows
- **Style:** Full-width rows divided by 1px Hairline; the label is Heading MD weight with 24px block padding and a 24px outlined chevron at the inline-end.
- **State:** The chevron turns 180° on open (320ms, standard ease); the answer is Charcoal body text capped near 68ch. Used for product details and FAQs.

### Comparison Table
- **Style:** Hairline row rules, start-aligned row labels in Body MD, Caption MD column heads in Mute. Inclusion is a 24px outlined tick in Ink; exclusion is a cross in Mute. Each mark carries an accessible label.

### Editions Shelf (signature)
A single row of book covers presented as a physical shelf.
- **Structure:** Each book is a Soft Cloud stage at 4:5 with the cover centred inside, followed by the book name in Body Strong and a release line in Caption MD Ink led by an 8px Bronze dot. A 1px Ink rule runs under the stages: the shelf the covers stand on.
- **Behaviour:** Scrolls inline with snap; a thin Hairline scrollbar. On hover-capable devices the cover (not the card, not the stage) rises 6px and tilts 1.2° over 600ms on the enter ease, and the Bronze dot scales up.
- **Entrance:** One staggered reveal on load (900ms per cover on the enter ease, 70ms between covers), only when the shelf starts on screen and motion is allowed. Content is visible without script.
- **Cover stand-in:** When a title has no image, a typographic cover fills the stage: a solid Ink, Accent Teal, Accent Pink Soft, Accent Pink Deep or Accent Purple Pale field with the title in the Arabic display face (Bronze on Ink), the author, and the house name under a 1px rule.

### Motion
State changes use the standard ease (cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)); arrivals use the enter ease (cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)); departures use the exit ease (cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)) and never entrances; presses use the press ease (cubic-bezier(0.3, 0, 0.5, 1)); linear is for marquee and shimmer loops only. Durations: 100ms press and chip flip, 200ms hover and focus, 320ms accordion and label swap, 600ms cover tilt and section reveal, 900ms campaign reveal, 70ms stagger, 24s marquee loop. Every duration drops to 0 under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

### Named Rules
**The One Primary Rule.** One Ink pill per screen, plus at most one Soft Cloud pill. There is no third button shape.

**The Still Card Rule.** Cards and stages never move or cast shadows. On a book, only the cover itself may tilt.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put every book image on Soft Cloud (#f5f5f5) at 4:5, filling its card edge to edge.
- **Do** use one Ink (#111111) primary pill per screen, with at most one Soft Cloud secondary pill.
- **Do** keep Canvas and Soft Cloud at roughly 95% of the interface and let covers and editorial imagery carry the colour.
- **Do** keep containers square (0px) and controls fully round (9999px).
- **Do** space on the 8px grid and step section gaps 48 / 32 / 24px across desktop, tablet and mobile.
- **Do** write layout in logical properties so RTL and LTR mirror cleanly.
- **Do** set Arabic display at a 1.25 line height so letters never clip.
- **Do** show focus as a 2px Ink outline at a 2px offset, Canvas on Ink.
- **Do** pair Success green with a word or icon, and give every tick or cross an accessible label.
- **Do** set the name آفاق الفكر as a text wordmark in the sans at weight 700, in Ink on light grounds or Canvas on Ink.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put shadows or lift on cards; the only shadows are the inset hairline and the search focus halo.
- **Don't** use sale red or the collection accents in base UI; red belongs to the price line only.
- **Don't** use Bronze for chrome or a CTA.
- **Don't** use accent colours for text or for a primary button.
- **Don't** use Charcoal as a button fill.
- **Don't** set Stone (#9e9ea0) on a light ground.
- **Don't** let a Hairline (#cacacb) border be the only boundary of a control.
- **Don't** use a display face for a section title or a book name, and don't uppercase Arabic or body text.
- **Don't** track letters; letter spacing stays 0.
- **Don't** underline anything but inline links and the active nav indicator.
- **Don't** invent, draw or generate a logo mark, set the wordmark in the display font, or add effects to it.
- **Don't** use emoji in the interface, or illustrations in place of photography and covers.
- **Don't** use left or right in layout; use inline-start and inline-end.
